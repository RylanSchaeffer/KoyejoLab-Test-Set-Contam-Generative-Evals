## Early phase-1 results (Finding #4 in accuracy space)

At fixed contamination, accuracy falls as overtraining rises — accuracy tracks loss, so
**Finding #4 holds and 8RFz's loss-vs-correctness objection is answered on its own terms**. The
"stealth contamination" alternative (accuracy persisting while loss rises) is not what happens.

| Model | Replicas | `ot` | Math Verify |
|---|---|---|---|
34M at R = 316, with the compute-optimal anchor merged in — monotone across the whole ladder:

| `ot` | 1 | 2 | 4 | 8 | 16 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Math Verify | 0.0732 | 0.0416 | 0.0254 | 0.0170 | 0.0134 |

And at higher contamination, where there is more to lose: 34M R=1000 falls 0.3867 (`ot=4`) to
0.1952 (`ot=8`); 34M R=3162 is 0.7984 at `ot=8`.

`CONTAMINATED_TOKEN_FRACTION.md` gives the mechanism: at fixed replica count, raising `ot`
raises the token budget, so the contaminated *share* falls (34M at R=316: 66.98% of tokens at
`ot=1` down to 4.19% at `ot=16`). "Overtraining dilutes contamination" is literal dilution.

A generation-time signal corroborates it: contaminated checkpoints finish in ~80 s because they
emit the memorized solution and stop; uncontaminated ones take ~1,050 s rambling to the
2,048-token cap.

## Notebook 16's number in the manuscript is stale (and the truth is better)

`04_further_training.tex:64` says SFT lowers perturbed-MATH NLL at **14/17 conditions, with
improvements up to -4.72 nats**. That prose was committed 2026-05-06 (`e0e8c52`). The
token-weighting bias in notebook 16's NLL aggregation was fixed on 2026-07-27 (`342deb5`),
*after* it. Re-running the notebook against the corrected aggregation gives:

- **17/17 conditions improved** — every single one, not 14 of 17.
- **Largest improvement -2.18 nats** (153M, R=1000), not -4.72.

So the consistency claim gets stronger and the magnitude claim gets weaker. Both numbers in
that sentence need updating; quoting -4.72 in the rebuttal would be quoting a pre-fix artifact.

Two further notes on this figure. The saved PNG on disk did **not** match what its own cached
data reproduces — same failure mode as notebook 11, so treat any committed figure as suspect
until regenerated. And the 344M curve is non-monotonic (R=100 gives -1.96, R=316 gives -0.78,
R=1000 gives -1.62); if that panel goes in the paper, that kink will draw a question and is
